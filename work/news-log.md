## Problems / caveats

- No paywalls blocked any candidate outright; all opened pages returned readable text. Several sites (nature.com, pubmed, etc.) were excluded by rule, not by access failure.
- Two accepted items are borderline aggregator/roundup style (theworldofai daily briefing and ai-hpc.org weekly watch for paper 3). Both have a written article section, a headline, a date, and a publication name, and both contain the paper's full title plus a nature.com paper link; they are flagged here so the submitter can decide whether to keep them.
- Three accepted items have links: [] because the paper link was present in the page but its exact target URL was not visible in the extracted page text (watchesreviewed, mediapavilion, ai-hpc). Their match rests on the full title (mediapavilion link text, ai-hpc body text) or the explicit "Nature paper" source link (watchesreviewed).
- For date-only publications (longevitydocs Nov 6, watchesreviewed Nov 13, ai-hpc Nov 9, liveinthefuture n/a) published_time was set to 00:00:00 UTC on that date, per the task rule.
- MedicalXpress author bylines: the extracted pages list authors under "Who's behind this story?" (Sadie Harley for both accepted MedicalXpress/Phys.org items, Andrew Zinin for the CRISPR item); those names are used as author.
- The evidence directory already contained two unrelated youtube_*.json files (youtube_01_smartwatch-hypertension-pawpress.json, youtube_02_autodna-gerwynn.json) from other work; they were not touched.
